THE CENTRAL IDEA

A surrogate is a problem, not merely a rough answer.

The pendulum is small enough to inspect and rich enough to make the point. Its linear approximation is elegant and analytical, but it erases amplitude-dependent timing. GHAM turns the transition from a known surrogate to the target system into an explicit mathematical object.

CLEAN DERIVATION

From deformation to computable corrections.

The correct setting is affine. Let \(X_0\) contain zero-initial-condition perturbations and \(X_A=\theta_0+X_0\) contain trajectories satisfying the prescribed initial conditions.

01 · FUNCTION-SPACE SETTING

Fixed initial conditions define an affine space.

\[ X_0=\{v\in C^2([0,T]):v(0)=0,\ \dot v(0)=0\}, \qquad X_A=\theta_0+X_0, \] \[ \mathcal N:X_A\to C([0,T]), \qquad \mathcal N[\theta]=\ddot\theta+\omega_0^2\sin\theta. \]
Why this matters

Every correction \(\theta_m\in X_0\) has homogeneous initial conditions, so every partial sum preserves \(\theta(0)=A\) and \(\dot\theta(0)=0\).

02 · ZERO-ORDER DEFORMATION

One equation contains the surrogate and the target.

\[ (1-q)\,\mathcal L[\Theta(t;q)-\theta_0(t)] =q\,\hbar\,\mathcal N[\Theta(t;q)], \qquad \mathcal L[v]=\ddot v+\omega_0^2v. \]
q = 0\(\mathcal L[\Theta-\theta_0]=0\)Homogeneous initial conditions imply \(\Theta(t;0)=\theta_0(t)\).
q = 1\(\hbar\mathcal N[\Theta]=0\)For \(\hbar\ne0\), the endpoint is the target equation.
Recovering the target solution from the series additionally requires convergence at \(q=1\).

03 · MACLAURIN SERIES IN THE EMBEDDING PARAMETER

Approximation order is independent of continuation.

\[ \Theta(t;q)=\theta_0(t)+\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\theta_m(t)q^m, \qquad \theta_m(t)=\left.\frac{1}{m!}\frac{\partial^m\Theta(t;q)}{\partial q^m}\right|_{q=0}. \] \[ \theta^{(M)}(t)=\sum_{m=0}^{M}\theta_m(t)\qquad\text{at }q=1. \]
\(\theta_0\)\(+\theta_1\)\(+\theta_2\)\(\cdots\)\(\theta^{(M)}\)

04 · FRÉCHET–TAYLOR EXPANSION

Expand the operator around the surrogate trajectory.

\[ \mathcal N[\theta_0+\Delta] =\mathcal N[\theta_0]+D\mathcal N[\theta_0](\Delta) +\frac{1}{2!}D^2\mathcal N[\theta_0](\Delta,\Delta)+\cdots, \] \[ \Delta=\sum_{m\ge1}\theta_mq^m, \qquad \mathcal N[\Theta(t;q)]=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}R_n(t)q^n. \]
The key move

Collect equal powers of \(q\). Each coefficient \(R_n\) depends only on already computed correction functions when solving the next deformation equation.

05 · m-TH ORDER DEFORMATION EQUATION

The nonlinear problem becomes a sequence of linear solves.

\[ \mathcal L[\theta_m-\chi_m\theta_{m-1}]=\hbar R_{m-1}, \qquad \chi_1=0,\quad \chi_m=1\ (m\ge2). \] \[ R_n=\left.\frac{1}{n!}\frac{\partial^n}{\partial q^n} \mathcal N[\Theta(\cdot;q)]\right|_{q=0}. \]
Computational payoff

The new unknown appears through the fixed linear operator \(\mathcal L\); nonlinear products contain only previous corrections.

01
Order is not a free lunch.

A higher \(M\) helps only while the correction sequence remains controlled over the chosen horizon.

02
\(\hbar\) changes the finite approximation.

The exact endpoint equation is unchanged, but the path and convergence region of the truncated series are not.

03
Restart when the local expansion is exhausted.

For large amplitudes or long horizons, restarting from a better local surrogate can be preferable to adding orders blindly.

WHY THIS TOY PROBLEM MATTERS

The same logic scales to sound-producing systems.

The pendulum teaches a general design rule: keep the phenomenon that carries information to the next stage. In audio, that may mean excitation, resonant body, memory, stability, or perceptually meaningful invariances.

oscillator→resonator→source–body model→full system→validated sound
